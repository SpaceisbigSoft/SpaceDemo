**SpaceDemo 0.9.0 beta 2 — a test version.** Watch old Counter-Strike 1.0–1.6 demos in today's Steam CS 1.6, with the original voice.

### Install
1. Download **SpaceDemo-Setup-0.9.0-beta.2.exe** below (Windows 10/11, 64-bit).
2. Windows SmartScreen may say *"Windows protected your PC"*: the installer isn't signed. Click **More info → Run anyway**.
3. You need **Steam** with **Counter-Strike** installed, and some old `.dem` demos (loose, in folders or in zips).

How to use it: the [user guide](https://github.com/SpaceisbigSoft/SpaceDemo/blob/main/docs/USER-GUIDE.md), or the **Help** page in the app.

### Testing? Please tell us
- Did it install and find Counter-Strike (bottom-left card says *Found*)?
- Did your demos show up in the Library, and did **Fix & watch** play them **with voice**? Note any demo that didn't, with its name and what you saw.
- Did the demo panel work (press `~` while the demo plays)?
- Optional: AI voice (Settings) and voice to text (a demo's Voice tab). If a note says *The AI voice couldn't start*, tell us.
- Anything confusing or broken: [open an issue](https://github.com/SpaceisbigSoft/SpaceDemo/issues) or tell the person who sent you this.

### Safe to use
SpaceDemo never changes your demos or the game's files, doesn't touch the running game, and starts demos through Steam with `-insecure` (VAC off for that session only).
