**SpaceDemo 0.9.0 beta 4 — a test version.** Watch old Counter-Strike demos in today's Steam CS 1.6, with the original voice: now **every version, back to the very first CS beta (beta 1.0, June 1999)**.

### New in beta 4
- **Demos from all the Counter-Strike betas now play.**
  - The **1999 betas** (beta 1.0–6.2), recorded on Half-Life 1.0.x with an older demo format (network protocols 38–40), are converted completely.
  - The **2000 betas** (beta 6.5–7.1, network protocols 41–42) play too.
  - The map, your gun and its animations show as they did. Beta-only models and sounds are swapped for today's: the "Arab" Terrorist becomes Leet, gun animations are matched to today's guns, and the suit's flatline plays when you die in the earliest betas.
- **Beta demos need the beta's own maps:** add the beta's `cstrike\maps` folder under Settings → Old map versions.
- Beta demos show in the **Library** and **Details** like any other demo. The Version column shows their protocol (for example *Old · p38*). Rounds also end on the round-end radio message, which the 1999 betas used.
- **What's new** in the update bar now lists everything that changed since the version you have, even across several releases.
- Fixes: a details tab that hits a problem now shows a short note instead of leaving the window blank. Demos the Library once listed as unreadable are read again.

### Updating from beta 2 or 3
SpaceDemo asks by itself a few seconds after it starts: click **Download**, then **Restart now**. Your library and settings stay. If no bar appears, go to Settings → Updates → **Check now**.

### New install
1. Download **SpaceDemo-Setup-0.9.0-beta.4.exe** below (Windows 10/11, 64-bit).
2. Windows SmartScreen may say *"Windows protected your PC"*: the installer isn't signed. Click **More info → Run anyway**.
3. You need **Steam** with **Counter-Strike** installed, and some old `.dem` demos (loose, in folders or in zips).

How to use it: the [user guide](https://github.com/SpaceisbigSoft/SpaceDemo/blob/main/docs/USER-GUIDE.md), or the **Help** page in the app.

### Safe to use
SpaceDemo never changes your demos or the game's files, doesn't touch the running game, and starts demos through Steam with `-insecure` (VAC off for that session only).
